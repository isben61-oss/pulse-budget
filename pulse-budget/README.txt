PulseBudget — Fullscreen Home-Screen Offline App (Android & iPhone)
===================================================================

Once installed to your home screen, PulseBudget opens in FULLSCREEN with
its own app icon and NO browser address bar or tabs — just like YouTube
— and runs 100% offline (even in Airplane Mode or after restarting your phone).

One-Time Setup (to get the fullscreen Home Screen icon):
1. Host this unzipped folder once on a local computer on your Wi-Fi
   (run `python3 -m http.server 8000` inside the folder) or any static
   host (e.g. GitHub Pages / Netlify Drop), then open that link once on your phone:
   - iPhone (Safari): Tap Share -> "Add to Home Screen" -> Add.
   - Android (Chrome): Tap Menu (⋮) -> "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
2. After that one-time install, close the browser and tap the PulseBudget
   icon on your home screen. It launches fullscreen, stores all data
   locally on your phone, and never needs the internet again.
